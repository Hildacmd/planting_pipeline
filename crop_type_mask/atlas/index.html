ICPAC Crop-Type Atlas

Stable 100 m crop-type masks for the 11 ICPAC member states — multi-year cropland vote × SPAM crop shares × suitability, calibrated to HarvestStat where statistics exist. Every band of the five GeoTIFF products, plus the 2024 maize season layers built on them, as ~1.3 km regional mosaics.

The five GeoTIFF products

One folder per country under crop_type_mask/<Country>/outputs/. All five share the same 100 m grid (EPSG:4326, 0.0009°), are Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs with NoData = 0 and carry band names. Every band of every product is a layer on the map above.

ProductWhat it holdsWhat it is for
<ISO>_crop_fraction_100m.tifOne band per crop: % of the cell under that cropThe layer to compute with. Area = Σ (fraction / 100 × pixel area); production = area × yield. Also the weight for district aggregates.
<ISO>_crop_mask_100m.tifmask_<crop> (0/1, crop ≥ 10 % of the cell and present in SPAM) and dominant (1 maize, 2 wheat, 3 sorghum, 4 teff, 5 millet)Restricting phenology, water balance, CPI and anomalies to land that plausibly grows the crop; dominant for display. Never count mask pixels as area.
<ISO>_crop_confidence_100m.tif0–100 per crop: cropland vote, SPAM presence, calibration and WorldCereal agreementStratifying or flagging results by evidence strength instead of dropping pixels silently.
<ISO>_crop_cropland_100m.tifcl_pct, vote_pct, agree_pct, nvotesThe cropland base every fraction is a share of, and how much the source maps disagreed — tighten or loosen the mask yourself.
<ISO>_pixel_area_ha_100m.tifTrue hectares per pixel (geodesic)Exact area and production on a geographic grid, where a pixel is never quite 1 ha (0.92–1.00 ha across the region).

Added on top: the 2024 maize season

Season monitoring for the eight countries with a 2024 maize run, from the ICPAC planting pipeline — what the crop masks are used for.

LayerWhat it holdsWhat it is for
Season WRSIFAO-56 water satisfaction over the season, 250 m maize pixels (Somalia: admin-2)Where water supply fell short of crop demand; the basis of the failure classes.
Crop Performance IndexRelative yield 0–100 from water, heat and vegetation stressThe season-quality signal behind yield and anomaly reporting.
Crop water stressSeason water deficit in mmHow much water the crop lacked — irrigation and drought messaging.
Maize yieldCPI × attainable ceiling calibrated to HarvestStat, by admin unitProduction estimates; Tanzania and South Sudan are uncalibrated and hatched.
Crop-failure riskASAP classes: share of each unit's maize area with WRSI < 50The unit-level alert trigger used in early-warning reporting.
