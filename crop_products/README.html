Crop product dossiers

One folder per crop. Each holds the products, a machine-readable status table, and a methodology dossier covering the method, what has been achieved, the gaps and the recommendations. All tables are generated by build_crop_dossiers.py from the shipped products — they cannot drift from what is actually in Earth Engine and on disk.
